Place your UPI payment QR image here as:

  payment-qr.png

The website loads it at /payment-qr.png

Do not commit this file if it contains sensitive merchant details you prefer to keep private;
add payment-qr.png to .gitignore and upload only on Vercel / hosting.

Registration fee: set REGISTRATION_FEE_INR=99 in Vercel environment variables.

After the job seeker pays, they submit email + UTR on the form → /api/register.
You verify the UPI credit in your bank app, then call /api/admin/verify_payment.

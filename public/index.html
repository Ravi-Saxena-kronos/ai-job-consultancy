Instructions — how this app works

Everything runs by email. We do not call you on phone. Follow these steps in order.

  1. Pay registration fee (₹99) Use GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or Amazon Pay. Pay to the UPI ID shown in Step 1 below. Save the UTR / reference number from your payment app.
  2. Register on this website Fill Step 2 with your name, email, and UTR. You get a Seeker ID (e.g. APS-XXXXXX). We match your UTR with our bank record. This usually takes a short time after payment.
  3. Wait for payment confirmation email When payment is verified, we email you at the address you gave. That mail has your Seeker ID and a link to upload your resume. You cannot upload until payment is verified.
  4. Upload resume and job preferences In Step 3, enter Seeker ID, same email, target role, experience, location, and a resume link (Google Drive or PDF URL anyone can open). We store your profile for matching.
  5. AI finds matching job openings Our system searches public job listings (for example via Adzuna) for roles that fit your role, location, and domain. We do not log in to LinkedIn or auto-apply on portals for you.
  6. We email companies only when careers email is verified For each match, we check for a verified company careers or HR email. If we find one, we send your application with your consent. If there is no verified email, we skip that company — you are not notified for skipped ones.
  7. You get email only when your resume was sent When your resume is actually emailed to a verified careers address, we notify you with company name and role reference. No email means we did not send to HR for that attempt.
  8. Track progress Your registration includes a limited number of company outreach attempts. Extra packs (5 / 10 / 15 companies) may be added later. All updates stay on email.

What we never do: guarantee interview or job, use LinkedIn login bots, or apply on job portals without a proper careers email path.
